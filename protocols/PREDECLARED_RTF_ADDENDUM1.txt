ADDENDUM 1 to PREDECLARED_RTF.txt. Written after the first two B1 label-permutation runs were seen to score clearly above 50 (B1 perm split 6: IR 78.9), and BEFORE any LF-PNCN permutation run and before any whole-bearing permutation run of any model. The original file is not changed.

Problem found in my own design (item 12 (i) of the pre-declaration): the declared permutation reassigns the faulty-end (IR, OR) label pair between bearings but leaves every healthy-end window at 0. Therefore, under ANY such permutation, "late-life window" stays correlated with the labels (IR is 1 for the late windows of 12 randomly chosen bearings, OR for 5), and a pure life-stage detector (early versus late life) obtains a held-out AUROC far above 50 on the permuted labels. The expectation "about 50, a mean above 56 indicates leakage" written in item 12 (i) is WRONG for this dataset. That control measures life-stage learning, not leakage or bearing-identity memorisation. The declared control is still run and reported as declared, with this interpretation.

SUPPLEMENTARY CONTROL (whole-bearing permutation, same idea as the PU and UORED audits): the faulty-end (IR, OR) label pair of each bearing is reassigned to another bearing as before, but ALL windows of a bearing, healthy-end and faulty-end, then carry the pair of the bearing it was assigned to. The labels are then constant per bearing and carry no life-stage information, so a model that does not memorise bearing identity into held-out bearings should score about 50 (the spread is large with 6 held-out bearings). Same 20 splits (5..24), 2 permutations per split, same redraw rule (at most 20 draws until train and test both contain both classes for both labels), seeds 8000 + 10 x split + k, all three models. Reading: held-out Macro mean above 56 for any model would indicate identity memorisation or leakage; the comparison of the three models is descriptive.

INTERPRETATION OF THE MAIN RESULTS: because "early versus late life" is a possible shortcut on this dataset, the late-life-negatives analysis (item 11, negatives restricted to late-life windows of bearings that lack the defect) is the more conservative fault-type measure and will be reported next to the primary result. The primary decision rules are unchanged.
