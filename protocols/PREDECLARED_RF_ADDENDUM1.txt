ADDENDUM 1 to PREDECLARED_RF.txt. Written after the primary random-forest results of all four data sets were seen (the original file is not changed), and before the diagnostic below was run.

This is a POST HOC DIAGNOSTIC, not part of the pre-declared decision rules. Reason: on the Paderborn run-to-failure data the primary random forest (85.7) scored at the level of the stage-only reference (84.1) and above all neural methods, and on XJTU-SY it was also above them. The 20 features mix 5 time-domain statistics (rms, peak-to-peak, kurtosis, skewness, crest factor), which are known to drift with bearing wear, with 15 spectral values. To see which block carries the score, the same primary random forest (identical settings, seed, splits, rows, metric) is trained on (a) the 5 time statistics only (feature columns 0-4) and (b) the 15 broadband envelope log-SNR values only (columns 5-19), once each, on all four data sets. Reported: mean per-split Macro-AUROC (and inner, outer) with the SD over splits, and for XJTU-SY and Paderborn run-to-failure also the stage-matched Macro (negatives = faulty-stage windows of bearings without the label, same definition and valid-split rule as the stage-matched analysis). Point estimates only, no bootstrap, no decision rule, no further variants and no tuning. Both subsets and nothing else are run; results are reported whatever they show.
