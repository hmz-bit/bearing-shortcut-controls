EXTERNAL VALIDATION ON CWRU (Case Western Reserve University Bearing Data Center, 12 kHz drive-end/fan-end fault files). Written before any model was trained on CWRU. Not to be changed afterwards.

Purpose: test, without any tuning, whether the PU results of LF-PNCN (`base`) and of the phase 2 change `rowz_drop` transfer to a third, independent dataset. Nothing on CWRU is used to choose or change a method. CWRU faults are ARTIFICIAL (electro-discharge-machined point defects of 7, 14, 21 mils); this is unlike PU's real bearings and UORED's naturally developed damage, and CWRU is known to be an easy benchmark (ceiling effects are possible).

FROZEN (identical to PU and UORED): network (LF-PNCN, 22,947 params), Tier 1 config (lr 1e-3, batch 64, weight decay 1e-4, 40 epochs, constant lr), Ball output excluded from the loss, standardiser from training windows only, median/MAD log-SNR order tensor (3 bands 500-2500, 2500-5000, 5000-10000 Hz, 5 harmonics, 64 xi points, Delta rule), variant definitions `base` and `rowz_drop` from scripts/phase2/variants.py (rowz_drop = per-window row z-score + dropout 0.3).

DATA HANDLING CHOSEN A PRIORI (same for both variants, not tuned):
 1. Splits: the 100 evaluation splits of Vieira et al. for CWRU in work/bearing-data-leakage/data/splits/cwru/eval: runs 3..52, each with two scenarios "DE" (train_DE/test_DE) and "FE" (train_FE/test_FE) = 100 splits; each (run, scenario) is trained once per variant. Tuning folds are not used. Train/test membership is taken from the waveform_id lists of those parquet files, never recomputed. Per split: 12 faulty bearings (unit = group = fault location_type_size) for training, 6 for testing, plus the healthy signals listed. The healthy signals of train and test come from different physical bearings (train_DE: healthy drive-end signals; test_DE: healthy fan-end signals; swapped in the FE scenario); this is asserted in code, as is the absence of any shared waveform or faulty group between train and test.
 2. Raw files: the 72 official 12 kHz files (ids from the official CWRU tables via their link targets; work/data/cwru/file_map.csv), 144 waveforms (drive-end and fan-end channels of each file). All are 12 kHz, so no resampling (the 48 kHz resampling of Vieira is not needed because the 12 kHz files only are used). File 279 (FE IR 0.007", 1 HP) has an RPM entry of 1732 inside the file, which disagrees with the table and with the shaft line; the nominal speed is used (below).
 3. Shaft frequency: f_r = nominal speed of the load condition / 60 (0 HP 1797, 1 HP 1772, 2 HP 1750, 3 HP 1730 rpm), the `rpm` column of the split metadata. Label-free check (results/cwru/speed_check.txt): the shaft line (Welch peak, 0.2 Hz resolution, mean of the DE and FE channel peaks) of 68 of 72 files lies within 0.6% of nominal; the other 4 are files 108 and 237 (0.8%, within the spectral resolution) and files 197 and 283 (one channel gave a spurious peak, -1.2% and -7.6% on the channel mean).
 4. Geometry by signal location (used for the fault-frequency orders of that signal): drive-end 6205-2RS JEM SKF z=9, d=7.94 mm, D=39.04 mm; fan-end 6203-2RS JEM SKF z=8, d=6.746 mm, D=28.50 mm; contact angle 0. Asserted to reproduce the BPFO/BPFI ratios of the split metadata (DE 3.5848/5.4152, FE 3.053/4.9469) to 3 decimals.
 5. Sampling: 12 kHz. The windowing and filters run at fs = 12 kHz; the third band 5000-10000 Hz is therefore truncated to 5000-5994 Hz by the existing band-capping code (Nyquist 6 kHz). This is a deviation from PU/UORED (42 kHz) and is stated as a caveat.
 6. Windows: 1 s (12000 samples), 75% overlap (hop 3000 samples), same for both variants, same a-priori rule as UORED; about 37 windows per 10 s signal.
 7. Labels: the signal-level `inner` and `outer` columns of the split metadata (1 only for the faulty-bearing signal of an inner-race or outer-race fault; healthy signals and ball-fault signals are 0 for both labels). Outer-race faults are the @6:00 position wherever available, otherwise @3:00 (as in Vieira).
 8. Seeds: seed = run number for the DE scenario, run + 1000 for the FE scenario. Device: CPU, 2 threads per process, several processes in parallel over disjoint runs.

MEASUREMENT: per split (run, scenario), window-pooled AUROC for the inner label and the outer label; Macro = mean of the two; mean over the 100 splits. Paired cluster bootstrap (B = 2000, seed 0, 95% percentile interval), weighted AUROC verified against sklearn. Clusters: each faulty bearing (group) for its faulty-signal windows; for healthy-signal windows the cluster is the experimental configuration in which they were recorded (group of the faulty file, prefixed "H_"), because the same two physical healthy bearings recur in many configurations; resampling strata: inner-fault groups (6), outer-fault groups (6), ball-fault groups (6), healthy clusters (18). Descriptive: per-faulty-bearing outer-race AUROC (that group's windows against all negatives of the splits where it is in the test set) and results by scenario.

PRE-STATED DECISION RULES (reported whatever the outcome):
 U1 (transfer): each variant's Macro-AUROC bootstrap lower bound > 50.
 U2 (the phase 2 gain replicates): rowz_drop minus base confirmed if difference > 0 and the 95% CI lower bound > 0; inconclusive if the point estimate >= 0 and the CI includes 0; not reproduced if the point estimate < 0. If both variants have Macro > 98, the result is labelled "ceiling effect, uninformative for U2" in addition.
 U3 (outer race): descriptive only, no threshold.
 No variant is added, removed or retuned after seeing results. If a pipeline error is found, it is fixed and ALL results rerun and both versions reported.

CAVEATS stated in advance: artificial EDM faults; 12 kHz with a truncated third band; two bearing geometries depending on the signal location; 18 faulty bearings in total; the healthy-cluster definition above treats configuration-level recordings of the same two healthy bearings as clusters (an understatement of healthy-side dependence is possible); CWRU is known to allow identification of the experimental configuration from signals, so high scores can reflect shortcuts; B1 (WDCNN) not run on CWRU; results are not comparable with Vieira's CWRU numbers (different label set and features).
